## Outreach

---

I joined the Organisation for Human Brain Mapping Communications Committee ([OHBMComCom](https://www.ohbm-com.com/)) in 2024 as a blog writer, and in 2026 took on the role of **Chair-Elect**. OHBMComCom provides accessible information about scientific advances in the field of human brain mapping and updates in the OHBM community via the OHBM Blog, NeuroSalience podcast, and Lay Media posts. 

As part of my role in OHBMComCom, I was invited to speak in a symposium dedicated to scientific communication and outreach at the OHBM 2026 Annual Meeting. Here, I discussed the key aspects of making scientific communication accessible to a broader audience in multiple formats, whether it be blogging, podcasting, or via social media. <img align="right" src="/content/media/OHBMComCom_image1.png">

I have also interviewed a number of eminent researchers for the OHBM Blog, including Profs Joana Cabral, Rogier Mars, and Mac Shine. Watch my conversation with Joana Cabral by clicking the thumbnail below. All interviews for the OHBM Blog can be found [here](https://www.ohbm-com.com/blog).
