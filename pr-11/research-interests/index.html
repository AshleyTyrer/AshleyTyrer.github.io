Research

Interoception

I investigate how people perceive and interpret signals from within the body, with a focus on cardiac and respiratory sensations and their role in health and behaviour.

Key Publications

Decision-making

My work examines the cognitive and neural processes that shape decisions, including how people learn from feedback and balance exploration with exploitation.

Dynamic Causal Modelling

I use Dynamic Causal Modelling to test mechanistic accounts of how brain regions interact, and how those interactions change across tasks and clinical conditions.

Metacognition

I study how people evaluate the quality of their own judgements, including the relationship between confidence, accuracy, and decision-making.
